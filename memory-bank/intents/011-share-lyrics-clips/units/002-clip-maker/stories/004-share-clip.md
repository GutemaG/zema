# Story: 004-share-clip

Share the video

**As a** listener
**I want** to send my clip to TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp or any app
**So that** my friends see it

- [ ] **Given** a finished video, **When** I tap Share, **Then** the system share menu opens with the MP4 and the text "title – artist"

- [ ] **Given** the share screen, **When** shown, **Then** a short note says platforms may mute or remove copyrighted music
- [ ] **Given** the editor closes, **When** it is gone, **Then** temporary frames and videos are deleted
